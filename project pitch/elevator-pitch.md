# Elevator Pitch

People trying to build healthier routines often keep notes in different places or have trouble seeing patterns over time. Wellness Calendar is a calendar-based wellness journal for recording nutrition and activity notes by day, setting goals, and reflecting on progress. For the MVP, I’ll focus on an end-to-end flow where a user can sign in, create and manage daily notes, and see them on a calendar backed by a relational database. The goal is to make wellness tracking organized and useful without making it feel complicated.
