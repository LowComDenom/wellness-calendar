# Wireframe Assessments

## 2026-10-30 - Main Calendar View (`Wireframe - Calendar v0.png`)

### Summary

The month grid is the primary focus, with a selected day's notes shown beside it. This is a promising structure for a calendar-based logging app: users can inspect a day without losing their place in the month.

### What works

- The calendar is visually dominant; secondary destinations are grouped in a menu.
- The expanded-day panel suggests that multiple notes can be reviewed for one date.
- The sketch indicates that monthly and weekly log views are part of the navigation model.

### Recommended changes

1. Add a year and clear previous/next month controls. The outlined shapes above and below the calendar are ambiguous; label their purpose.
2. Add weekday headings and show leading/trailing dates so the grid maps to real calendar weeks.
3. Explain the small circle on the first date and define other date indicators for notes, goals, today, and selection.
4. Add an obvious way to create a note or goal from a selected date, plus an empty-day state.
5. Clarify the monthly and weekly log controls, including their hit areas and destinations. The diamond's meaning is not self-evident.
6. Account for busy days with many notes and narrow screens, where the side panel may need to become a drawer or stacked section.
7. Make date and view controls keyboard-operable, with visible focus and selected states.

### Suggested next iteration

Annotate four states: month with no selected date, selected date with notes, selected empty date, and a date with many notes. Include the month/year controls and label each date indicator before refining visual styling.
